## Natural Earth (basemap)

`raw/natural_earth/` – 1:10m countries, admin-1 lines, rivers and lakes from
https://www.naturalearthdata.com (public domain), clipped to Europe (12°W–35°E, 35–65°N)
to keep the files small.
